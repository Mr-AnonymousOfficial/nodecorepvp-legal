# Privacy Policy for Nodecore Arena

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026  
**Provider:** Nodecore Development®  
**Contact / Support:**  https://discord.gg/2cBj8y6guq or nodecoredevelopment@gmail.com

---

### 1. Overview
Nodecore Arena operates as an integrated service across Discord and FiveM game servers. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, how it is processed, and how your data is protected when interacting with our Discord bot and in-game resources.

---

### 2. Data We Collect

#### A. Discord Data (Collected via Discord API)
* **User Identifiers:** Discord User ID, Username, Global Name, and Avatar URL.
* **Guild Data:** Server ID, Channel IDs, and Role IDs where the bot operates.
* **System Logs:** Command execution timestamps, interaction metrics, and error/debug logs.

#### B. FiveM / In-Game Data (Collected via FiveM Scripts & Server Engine)
* **Player Identifiers:** FiveM License ID, Steam ID, Discord ID, and IP address (logged temporarily for connection verification and network security).
* **Gameplay Metrics:** K/D ratios, match scores, leaderboard rankings, arena playtime, and tournament/ranked progression.
* **Virtual Assets:** Arena inventory items, earned in-game currency, and cosmetic preferences.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Data
We process collected data strictly for the following operational purposes:
* **Account Synchronization:** Linking Discord profiles with FiveM player accounts to sync roles, access tiers, and competitive leaderboards.
* **Core Gameplay Features:** Managing matchmaking queues, calculating live scores, and displaying server rankings.
* **Security & Moderation:** Preventing exploits, managing ban lists, and enforcing fair play across platforms.
* **Maintenance & Optimization:** Analyzing performance logs, database query speeds, and system errors to improve uptime.

> **Data Promise:** Nodecore Development® does **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

---

### 4. Storage, Security & Retention
* **Database Security:** Data is stored in secure, restricted MariaDB/MySQL databases protected by access control layers.
* **Data Retention:**
  * **Gameplay & Profile Data:** Retained as long as your account remains active to maintain historical stats and leaderboard standing.
  * **System & Debug Logs:** Automatically purged every 30 days.

---

### 5. Third-Party Integrations
Our services interact directly with official third-party platforms and APIs. By using Nodecore Arena, you acknowledge that your interactions are also subject to their respective terms and privacy guidelines:

* **Discord API:** Governed by the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) and [Discord Developer Terms](https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/terms-of-service).
* **Cfx.re / CitizenFX Collective (FiveM):** Governed by the [Cfx.re Terms of Service](https://fivem.net/terms) and [Cfx.re Privacy Policy](https://fivem.net/privacy).
* **Rockstar Games:** Subject to the [Take-Two Interactive / Rockstar Games Privacy Policy](https://www.take2games.com/privacy) regarding GTA V network identifiers.

---

### 6. User Rights & Data Removal
In accordance with GDPR and global privacy standards, you maintain the right to access or request the complete erasure of your personal data.

* **Deletion Requests:** You can request full data erasure by opening a support ticket in our official Discord server.
* **Impact of Erasure:** Deleting your data will permanently wipe all in-game progress, leaderboard rankings, and linked account settings across Nodecore Arena services.
